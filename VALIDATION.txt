GUIDE 4.2 — THE LAST SIX GAPS CLOSED
------------------------------------
Six features that had been listed as untaught since v3.7 are now chapters. Each
was read from the SOURCE before being written, because a guide that describes a
feature approximately is worse than one that omits it.

TAUGHT, each with its limits and not only its benefit:
  Impact meter        — 8 tracked events; the COUNTS are real, the per-event
                        minutes are an assumption editable in Settings, and the
                        result is labelled an estimate everywhere it appears.
  Gainsight queue     — real, persists, exports as JSON; the transport
                        DELIBERATELY does not exist. Taught as a staging area,
                        not a push. Storing credentials readies the seam without
                        opening it.
  Signal calibration  — refuses to score a signal under 4 resolutions, and
                        excludes recommendations that resolved on their own,
                        because those are not a verdict on the advice.
  Account Brief       — inline Gainsight clearing is the IN-FLOW surface; the
                        To-Do tab is the BATCH surface with "Copy all & queue".
                        Same list, two moments.
  AI Call Prep        — states what slice of the history it was built from, and
                        flags a response that hit the length cap.
  Progress Report     — a snapshot, not a live link; carries TTFV, pending
                        Gainsight count, and the labelled impact estimate.

FIXED IN THE TRACKER WHILE WRITING THIS — the Today close button read "Review and
       push all N", claiming a push the app cannot perform. syncGainsightQueue()
       always returns transport_not_built, and the Risk tab says so plainly
       ("queue ready, transport not yet connected ... Nothing is ever sent
       silently"). The close now reads "Review all N" and states that nothing is
       sent automatically. Documenting the feature is what exposed the overclaim.

PASS — 17 rhythm cards render; all six new chapters present; zero JS errors;
       compat "Bundled file confirmed"; no text anywhere claims an automatic push.
