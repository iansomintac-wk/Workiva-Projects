## Note for the team

The FR archetypes were written top-down (what we expect to hear) and the evidence base is thin: two customer-specific patterns, five hypotheses. That is fine for a first version, but the "what customers actually describe" chapter should be read as a listening guide, not proof. Two more first-person quotes per archetype from FR-specific calls would move the strongest ones to EMERGING or STRONG.
