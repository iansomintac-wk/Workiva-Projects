two shortcuts worth muscle memory

Prompt Advisor

Ready-to-use prompts organized by industry — search, filter, fill in the brackets, customize. It exists to kill the blank page. So never say "you need to learn prompt engineering." Say:

"You don't have to start from scratch. Let's see if there's already a prompt close to what you're trying to do, and customize it."

Nuance: in the Document companion, Advisor prompts don't automatically take the document's context the way a custom prompt referencing the document can. When the document's content matters, reference it explicitly.

Knowledge bases

For you: instead of memorizing every procedural step, ask Workiva Support Intelligence and verify with the linked sources.

For customers: instead of asking generic AI "what does ESRS E1 require?", select ESRS or ISSB Intelligence and interrogate the framework there.

Responses include a Sources section — teach "ask → inspect source → act," never "ask → trust."

teach the limits as clearly as the capabilities

ai output is a draft, an analysis aid, a reviewer — not the final authority

accuracy — why it matters right now

Workiva tells users to review AI output and not rely on it as the source of current laws or regulations. Standards move: the EC adopted revised ESRS in July 2026 — they show as Pending in Sustainability Explorer while the 2023 Delegated Act remains Current, applying from FY2027 with 2026 early-adoption options.

Say: "Let's use AI to accelerate understanding and drafting — then verify the underlying requirement and version."

security — the precise talk track

"Workiva states customer data is not used to train Workiva AI or third-party models, and AI respects the platform's authorization controls. We should still follow your organization's data-handling and AI policies and avoid supplying sensitive information unnecessarily."

That's stronger than "nothing is stored" or "zero data processing" — claims that don't match Workiva's current FAQ.

the prompt that writes the prompt

For beginners, this changes the psychology from "I have to know how to prompt" to "I only have to know what I'm trying to accomplish."

have them type

"I need Workiva AI to help me with [task]. Ask me only the questions you need to create an effective prompt. After I answer, return the final prompt I should use."

what a successful ai moment looks like

Real customer task + real source context + visible output + one refinement + repeat-use discussion.

Better than a CRM field that says "Workiva AI discussed." Weight first real task and repeat use most — exposure isn't behavior change.
