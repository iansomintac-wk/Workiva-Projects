# Workiva AI: Overview, Prompting Best Practices, and Quick Wins

Workiva AI is a secure, **integrated AI assistant** built into the Workiva platform for financial reporting, GRC, and sustainability workflows.  It provides a global “chat” assistant and **file-specific companions** (in documents/spreadsheets) that draw context from your Workiva data.  Because it operates within Workiva’s secure platform, *no customer data ever leaves the environment*.  (An Org Admin signs the Workiva AI Terms of Use to enable it; it’s encrypted in transit and never trained on your proprietary data.)

Workiva AI’s interface includes: a chat window (accessible anywhere, even mobile) and **embedded companions**.  In a document or spreadsheet, the companion panel can **read your current file** and allow inline edits or content generation.  You can ask it questions, use pre-built “quick edits” or prompts, and even attach reference files (like PDFs or DOCX) for analysis.  For example, selecting a table in a sheet and asking Workiva AI to summarize it yields a natural-language narrative of the data.  Similarly, the **SEC Filing Intelligence** feature lets financial teams load peer 10‑K/10‑Q filings and ask AI to compare disclosures or highlight trends.

## Prompting Best Practices

Effective prompting is crucial.  Workiva’s documentation emphasizes being **clear and specific**: explicitly state your task, mention topic/audience, desired length/style, etc..  For instance, instead of a vague “write remediation steps”, try: *“You are a GRC analyst. Using the evidence from [Control Failure Document], write a detailed remediation strategy for the accounts payable control failure outlined.”*  The docs advise providing **context** in your prompt.  You can reference text from a file or load it via File Intelligence, or explain the scenario in your prompt. For example, prefacing a request with role/context yields better results.

Best practices include:
- **One question at a time**: Ask singular, focused questions to get precise answers. If needed, follow up iteratively.
- **Iterate and refine**: If the answer is off, adjust the prompt or add detail and try again. Use the AI response to guide your next prompt.
- **Use the Prompt Advisor**: Workiva includes a Prompt Library with sample prompts (e.g. “Year-over-Year Financial Narrative”) to adapt for your needs.
- **Select the right knowledge base**: By default, Workiva AI uses a general knowledge base.  For more accuracy on Workiva features or standards, switch to a specialized KB (like *Workiva Support Intelligence*) so responses pull from trusted documentation. 

Following these guidelines helps teammates who are new to AI: encourage them to **write full sentences**, include background, and treat the AI like a knowledgeable coworker.

## Quick-Win Use Cases for GRC

Workiva AI can dramatically speed up common GRC tasks.  For example:

- **Control-gap and coverage analysis**: Load your controls and risk framework and ask the AI to identify overlaps or missing mappings.  In a demo, a user prompted Workiva AI with the enterprise *risk and control matrix* and it quickly flagged duplicate controls and suggested how to consolidate them.  A starter prompt could be:  
  *“Review my control matrix for duplicate or overlapping controls and suggest consolidation steps.”*

- **Risk factor drafting**: AI can draft or validate risk disclosures. E.g. “Draft a material risk factor based on [annual report text] focusing on market volatility.”

- **Flowcharting processes**: Workiva’s GRC solution now supports an AI-powered flowchart visualizer.  It can take a textual process narrative (e.g. “accounts payable workflow”) and generate a process flow diagram.  This helps audit teams visualize controls. (During an internal demo, Workiva AI generated a flowchart from a process description and allowed interactive linking of new risks to steps.)

- **Evidence analysis**: *Planned feature:* an **Evidence Analyzer** (demoed as “coming soon”) will scan attached audit evidence files and automatically confirm if the requested information is present.  This would save manual file review. (Even now, you can ask AI questions like “Does file X contain evidence for control Y?” after referencing it.)

- **Controls documentation**: Quickly draft control statements or test procedures.  For example, select a control description text and ask AI to rewrite it in compliance-friendly language or to list audit steps.

- **GRC Chatbot Scenarios**: The chat can answer general GRC questions. For instance, load your regulatory framework and ask: “What regulations impact our [industry] controls?”

These make easy wins because they fit into existing workflows. They use data you already have in Workiva (controls, risks, audit files). 

## Quick-Win Use Cases for Financial Reporting

Financial reporting teams can also benefit immediately:

- **Narrative generation from data**: Select rows/columns in a spreadsheet or table in a report and ask Workiva AI to “Summarize this table” or “Explain the key variance drivers.” It will produce draft narrative text. For example, selecting an income statement table and asking, *“Explain the year-over-year change in revenue”*, yields a coherent explanation.

- **Disclosures drafting and checking**: Use the AI companion to help write footnotes or MD&A. You can prompt it like, *“Draft a revenue recognition footnote based on our policies and last year’s disclosure”*.  It can also QA existing text: e.g. highlight a paragraph in a draft and use a quick-edit prompt “clarify this paragraph” or “check this against ASC 606”.

- **SEC filing analysis**: With the SEC Filing Intelligence panel, financial teams can query peer filings. For instance, filter peer 10-Ks and ask “How did our EBITDA margin compare to peers?” or “List any peer disclosures about supply chain risks.” This surfaces insights from real SEC data.

- **Data consistency (Tie-out)**: Workiva plans a **Tie-Out Agent** that automates consistency checks across schedules and reports. It will flag any mismatched figures instantly, linking each discrepancy to its source. While the full agent may require a premium, even today a user can ask the companion to “Check if the total assets figure in this document matches the sum of schedules X and Y” after referencing the relevant data. 

- **Benchmarking**: The *Benchmarking Agent* (available in advanced AI packages) analyzes peer SEC filings to find disclosure gaps. For a quick win, a user can load two or three peer 10-Ks and ask “What common revenue recognition language do competitors use?” or “Are we missing any risk factors compared to peers?” 

- **SEC questions**: If preparing an 10-K or 8-K, a team can load draft comments from senior management and ask the AI “Rewrite this earnings release quote in a formal tone” or “Summarize this analyst call transcript for key financial highlights.”

Overall, quick wins come from tasks that are **repetitive and text-heavy** – drafting, explaining, or checking data. Highlighting these in meetings (with short demos if possible) will show customers immediate value.

## 30-45 Min Internal Training Agenda

**Objective:** Equip the team with AI fundamentals and relatable demos so they can confidently talk to customers. An example agenda:

1. **Introduction to Workiva AI (5 min)** – Briefly explain what Workiva AI is and its governance. Emphasize **security and context** (cite how data stays in Workiva). Show the chat icon and companion panel in a slide.
2. **Prompting Best Practices (10 min)** – Cover key tips: be specific, provide context, ask one thing at a time, iterate. Use real examples (e.g. show an “ineffective vs effective” prompt, as in Workiva docs). Maybe do a short live/example prompt in front of a document.
3. **Demo: Workiva AI in Action (10 min)** – Walk through a quick demo screen (if possible) or recorded video: show using the Intelligent Companion in a document (e.g. summarizing a table) and the chat analyzing a file. Illustrate selecting a different knowledge base. Emphasize ease of use.
4. **Use Cases – GRC (5 min)** – List top AI features for audit/risk teams: control mapping, evidence analysis, process flowcharts. Use bullet points with citations (e.g. “Analyze control matrix for duplicates”). Optionally show a screenshot or clip of the flowchart generator.
5. **Use Cases – Financial Reporting (5 min)** – List AI use cases for finance: narrative drafting, data tie-out, peer benchmarking. Quote the Tie-Out Agent capabilities and SEC filing analysis. Show a short video clip if available (from Demo Center) of Workiva AI reviewing a financial table.
6. **Starter Prompts and Resources (5 min)** – Share example prompts on a slide (e.g., *“Identify 3 ways to shorten this paragraph”*, *“Outline audit steps for control X”*). Point to resources: Workiva’s Prompt Library, demo videos, and Support articles (Workiva AI FAQ, Tips page). Mention the Demo Center for quick customer video links.

**Total**: ~35–45 minutes. Leave time for questions and encourage the team to try the demo center and knowledge base links afterwards.

Embedding quotes (like “Workiva AI helped us save 40% of the time on our annual report”) builds credibility. 

By combining Workiva’s guidance on prompting with real use-case examples from release notes and demos, your team can confidently coach customers to use AI. The key is to start small (simple prompts, quick tasks) and show immediate value, reinforcing that Workiva AI is **safe, integrated, and designed for their high-stakes workflows**.
