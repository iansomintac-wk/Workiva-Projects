the one idea to teach

Workiva AI is most useful when you give it a real sustainability task, the data, disclosure, or framework it should read, and a clear picture of what a useful answer looks like.

Quick wins come from tasks that are repetitive and text-heavy: drafting, explaining, and checking. Your job isn't to teach "AI" — it's to find one of those tasks in the customer's reporting cycle and remove part of it during the call.

the shift

software that helps → software that does the work

Enterprise software has always run on one model: you do the work and the software helps by organizing, connecting, and speeding things up. AI breaks that model. Software can now understand what needs to happen and do it.

old model

The customer’s team decides in a meeting that you need to know why Scope 3 emissions spiked in one region. That becomes an action item.

new model

The software is already pulling the data, comparing periods, and drafting the analysis before the meeting ends.

the line that never moves

traceable. governed. defensible.

More automation means more decisions about how much control to hand over, and the customer makes that choice. Self-driving on a familiar route in good weather feels fine; on an icy mountain road you want the wheel back. Every AI action explains itself, respects permissions, and holds up to an audit. That isn’t a constraint on the AI story. It is the AI story.

“More of the work gets done, you stay in control, and you can trust the result.” Julie Iskow, President & CEO

5.3 million

Means nothing on its own. Add tonnes of CO₂e, which scope, which period, which boundary, how it compares to the target, and suddenly it can be analyzed, explained, and questioned. Workiva AI is moving from the value in a cell to understanding what that number is, where it came from, and what it connects to. That understanding is what lets it take on more of the work well, not just faster.

what
workiva ai is

A secure, integrated assistant built into the Workiva platform. Not a separate chatbot you paste into — it reads the report, emissions data, or disclosure you're already working in.

three places it shows up

point to these in a demo

Global chat

Available anywhere in the platform, including mobile. Ask questions, brainstorm, attach reference files (PDF, DOCX) for analysis, and iterate turn by turn.

Best for: questions, first drafts, working through a problem.

File companions

A panel inside Documents and Spreadsheets that reads the current file. Select a table or paragraph, then edit inline, generate content, or use pre-built quick edits.

Best for: tightening narrative, summarizing metric tables, checking consistency.

ESRS / ISSB Intelligence

A knowledge base grounded in the ESRS and ISSB standards. Ask what a requirement demands, who should own the evidence, and what to retain, with cited sources.

Best for: framework questions, requirement-to-workflow translation, evidence planning.

the three-question discovery pattern
1 · What takes time?

"What is something in this workflow you repeatedly spend 20 to 60 minutes doing?"

2 · What is the source of truth?

"Where is the information you use to do it: the report Document, a factbook, a spreadsheet, prior-year disclosures, supplier files, or the accounting guidance?"

3 · What does "done" look like?

"When you’re finished, what have you produced: a variance summary, a disclosure draft, an anomaly list, a reviewer comment list?"

Those three answers almost directly populate Source, Context, Objective, and Parameters in SCOPE. And whenever a pain surfaces, don't stop at the outcome: ask "walk me through how you do that today." That one question separates a vague pain from an investigable workflow.

what a successful ai moment looks like

Real customer task + real source context + visible output + one refinement + repeat-use discussion.

Better than a CRM field that says "Workiva AI discussed." Weight first real task and repeat use most. Exposure isn't behavior change.

the moment to aim for

A sustainability lead selects an emissions table in their own report, gets a variance analysis that matches the numbers, tells the AI to separate data-quality from operational causes, and says "we could use this before every assurance cycle."

what drives adoption

Freedom to experiment, role-specific applications, and a prompt library they can start from. Not abstract AI mastery.
