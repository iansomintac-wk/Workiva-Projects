the one idea to teach

Workiva AI is most useful when you give it a real reporting task, the source data or document it should read, and a clear picture of what a useful answer looks like.

Quick wins come from tasks that are repetitive and text-heavy: drafting, explaining, and checking. Your job isn't to teach "AI" — it's to find one of those tasks in the customer's close and remove part of it during the call.

the shift

software that helps → software that does the work

Enterprise software has always run on one model: you do the work and the software helps by organizing, connecting, and speeding things up. AI breaks that model. Software can now understand what needs to happen and do it.

old model

The customer’s team decides in a meeting that you need to know why revenue moved in one segment. That becomes an action item for the controller.

new model

The software is already pulling the two periods, comparing them, and drafting the variance narrative before the meeting ends.

the line that never moves

traceable. governed. defensible.

More automation means more decisions about how much control to hand over, and the customer makes that choice. Self-driving on a familiar route in good weather feels fine; on an icy mountain road you want the wheel back. Every AI action explains itself, respects permissions, and holds up to an audit. That isn’t a constraint on the AI story. It is the AI story.

“More of the work gets done, you stay in control, and you can trust the result.” Julie Iskow, President & CEO

5.3 billion

Means nothing on its own. Add revenue, for which period, which segment, how it compares to last year, and suddenly it can be analyzed, explained, and questioned. Workiva AI is moving from the value in a cell to understanding what that number is, where it came from, and what it connects to. That understanding is what lets it take on more of the work well, not just faster.

what
workiva ai is

A secure, integrated assistant built into the Workiva platform. Not a separate chatbot you paste into — it reads the document, spreadsheet, or filing you're already working in.

three places it shows up

point to these in a demo

Global chat

Available anywhere in the platform, including mobile. Ask questions, brainstorm, attach reference files (PDF, DOCX) for analysis, and iterate turn by turn.

Best for: questions, first drafts, working through a problem.

File companions

A panel inside Documents and Spreadsheets that reads the current file. Select a table or paragraph, then edit inline, generate content, or use pre-built quick edits.

Best for: summarizing tables, drafting footnotes, tightening MD&A.

SEC Filing Intelligence

Load peer 10-Ks and 10-Qs and ask the AI to compare disclosures, surface trends, or list the language competitors use.

Best for: benchmarking, disclosure gap checks, peer research.

the three-question discovery pattern
1 · What takes time?

"What is something in this workflow you repeatedly spend 20 to 60 minutes doing?"

2 · What is the source of truth?

"Where is the information you use to do it: the 10-K document, a schedule, a spreadsheet, prior-year filings, peer filings, or the accounting guidance?"

3 · What does "done" look like?

"When you’re finished, what have you produced: a variance narrative, a footnote draft, a tie-out checklist, a reviewer comment list?"

Those three answers almost directly populate Source, Context, Objective, and Parameters in SCOPE. And whenever a pain surfaces, don't stop at the outcome: ask "walk me through how you do that today." That one question separates a vague pain from an investigable workflow.

The platform anticipates obligations before anyone asks, flagging a new filing requirement the moment an entity’s ownership structure changes. Reporting platform becomes the platform for regulatory work.

what a successful ai moment looks like

Real customer task + real source context + visible output + one refinement + repeat-use discussion.

Better than a CRM field that says "Workiva AI discussed." Weight first real task and repeat use most. Exposure isn't behavior change.

the moment to aim for

A controller selects a table in their own 10-K draft, gets a variance narrative that matches the numbers, tells the AI to lead with gross margin, and says "we could use this every quarter."

what drives adoption

Freedom to experiment, role-specific applications, and a prompt library they can start from. Not abstract AI mastery.
