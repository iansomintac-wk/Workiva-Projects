## Access rules
- Included once the organization has signed the Workiva AI Terms of Use; admins can limit it to some workspaces or users.
- Solution-dependent features need the right Workiva solutions; previews go to selected customers.
- Chat takes no uploads; files must be in the workspace first, up to 10 per prompt (S3, S6).
- Spreadsheets return a formula, not a calculated value (S19).
