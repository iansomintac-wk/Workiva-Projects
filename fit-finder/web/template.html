    How it rates

    The rule

    Good fits recur and are mostly reading, comparing, checking, summarizing, drafting or rewriting text and tables. Judgment and sign-off, exact calculation, work in other systems and chasing people stay with you. Workiva AI does a first pass; a person reviews it.

    How accurate it is

    Language patterns, no AI service. On 60 held-out task descriptions written in customers' voices it matched careful labeling 75% of the time. It has not yet been measured on real customers' tasks, so treat the rating as a starting point.

    What it does not claim

    It describes what generative AI can do with your text, not specific Workiva features or packages. Ask your Workiva team which capabilities your license includes.
