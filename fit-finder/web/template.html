TaskFit
How it rates

Good fits come back every period and are mostly reading, comparing, checking, summarizing or drafting. Judgment, exact calculation, other systems and chasing people stay with you. It is pattern-based and uses no AI.

On 60 sample tasks written and labelled by AI it agreed on fit 75% of the time. On 19 real prompts (Workiva and community posts) the first feature it suggested was right for 15. On 29 newer real prompts, which it was tuned on and which AI labelled, it suggested a wrong feature 5 times in 23 that fit, and agreed on fit for only 12 of 29. On 40 new test sentences aimed at weak spots, written and labelled by AI and never tuned on, the first feature was right for 11 of 29 that fit, wrong for 9, and a feature was shown for 4 tasks that do not fit. So treat it as a first guess and check the linked support page. Features come from 23 Workiva support pages read on 15 Oct 2026; 22 of 23 re-checked on 18 Oct 2026 (one needs sign-in).

Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your tasks and files are not uploaded or sent to any AI service; file readers load from a public CDN only when you add that file type.
