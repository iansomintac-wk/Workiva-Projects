Team demo walkthrough

Five steps against a practice connector on this computer. Nothing here talks to a real system.

The live Gainsight and Snowflake connection waits on Ian's internal team (credentials and access); until then the tracker works fully offline and by file import, and this practice connector sends nothing anywhere.

Each Open the tracker button loads a fake practice book (17 made-up accounts) and a made-up send queue on this computer only. If the tracker already holds accounts it asks before replacing them; say Cancel and your book stays untouched.

Checking the connector…

  1. Check for calls

    Open the tracker and run the call check: it shows 5 matched and 1 no-account.

    What the real connector does: asks Gong for recent calls and matches each one to an account in your book.

    Open the tracker

  2. Read Gainsight accounts

    In the tracker, read the Gainsight accounts and see the account list fill in.

    What the real connector does: reads company records from Gainsight; it never changes them in this step.

    Open the tracker

  3. Check CSAT

    In the tracker, choose Check CSAT, look at the dry run, then choose Apply.

    What the real connector does: reads survey scores from Snowflake, read-only, and the tracker applies them only after you confirm.

    Open the tracker

  4. Send to Gainsight

    In the tracker, send the prepared updates: it shows 2 sent and 1 failed.

    What the real connector does: writes the updates to Gainsight one at a time and reports which ones were refused.

    Open the tracker

  5. Health

    In the tracker, open the connector health line. It names each adapter as fake or unproven.

    What the real connector does: reports which adapters are live, and marks any it has not yet proven against your tenant as unproven.
