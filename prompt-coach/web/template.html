How the coach grades

The rule

Weak: the AI would have to guess what to read or what you want back. Better: it names a source and one job. Strong: all five SCOPE elements, and one job at a time.

How it works

About 200 language patterns spot each element, and a small scoring model trained on 1,080 labeled reporting, GRC and sustainability prompts weighs them. It all runs on this page; there is no AI service behind it.

How accurate it is

On 320 held-out prompts it matched careful human-style grading 87% of the time, and nearly never confuses weak with strong. It is a coach, not a judge: if it misses something you wrote, rephrase it more plainly or override the task type.

Where the criteria come from

  • Workiva: tips for useful responses
  • Workiva: reference files in chat
  • Microsoft: goal, context, source, expectations
  • Anthropic: be clear and direct

Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your prompt and files are not uploaded or sent to any AI service; file readers load from a public CDN only when you add that file type. Rewrites are templates filled from your words and files: check them against your source before you use them, and follow your organization's AI policy.
