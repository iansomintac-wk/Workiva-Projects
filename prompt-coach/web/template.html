How the coach grades

The rule

Weak: the AI would have to guess what to read or what you want back. Better: it names a source and one job. Strong: all five SCOPE elements, and one job at a time.

How it works

About 200 language patterns spot each element, and small scoring models trained on 1,080 labeled reporting, GRC and sustainability prompts weigh them and recognize the kind of task. Each element is credited only from its own words. It all runs on this page; there is no AI service behind it.

How accurate it is

On 320 held-out prompts it matched careful human-style grading 86% of the time, and nearly never confuses weak with strong. It is a coach, not a judge: if it misses something you wrote, rephrase it more plainly.

Where the criteria come from

  • Workiva: tips for useful responses
  • Workiva: reference files in chat
  • Microsoft: goal, context, source, expectations
  • Anthropic: be clear and direct
