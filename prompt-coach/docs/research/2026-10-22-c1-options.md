# C1 miss: what blocks it and what each fix would do (2026-10-22, close-out session 19888854)

The fresh strong-30 (data/done_when_strong.jsonl) was not opened or run for any of this; the throwaway
copy used for the spike left that file out. r1 (data/done_when_strong_r1.jsonl) is the seen dev set.
Probes are in the session's tmp folder (c1probe.js, c1probe2.js, c1probe3.js); nothing below changed the repo.

## Measured: the cause

- 10 of the 13 r1 misses fail on Context. Every one of them has **zero Context rule hits**. Their score of
  0.661 is the model's starting value with no evidence. Weak prompts with no Context cue sit at the same
  0.661, so no cut-off can split them: lowering C's "sure" cut-off from 0.70 to anything between 0.55 and 0.66
  moves r1 from 18 to 28/30 (sure-gate only) but sim false "sure" from 3 to 16 of 111 (14.4%).
- The gap is general, not specific to r1. Training rows labeled Context=1 with zero Context hits: 99 of 532
  (53 of those are labeled 1 on every letter). Test 32/149, sim 17/34.
- The Context detector says "present" almost always: test precision 0.54, recall 0.996, accuracy 0.585
  (eval.js on a control retrain).
- What the user sees for the 13 r1 misses: 12 are graded "strong", headline "It names a source, context, one
  job, constraints, and a check.", Better and Strong left untouched, but with no "No changes needed" line.
  One (r1#21, "For procurement leadership: ...") is graded better and gets "This is for the reader of this report." added.
- Hidden twin of the problem: weak prompts graded "strong" and left untouched (no label) today: test 15/247
  (6.1%), sim 17/111 (15.3%).

## Bar statistics (computed)

Chance a coach passes 27/30 by luck, by its true rate: 70% -> 0.9%, 80% -> 12.3%, 90% -> 64.7%, 95% -> 93.9%.
A truly 90% coach fails this bar about 1 time in 3, so 30 rows is a coarse check; 100+ rows would be firmer.
For a "no changes" cap of 5% held with 95% confidence, at least 59 weak prompts are needed (order-statistic rule,
Tong et al. 2018, arXiv 1608.03109, abstract read).

## Outside patterns (research notes beside this file; mostly docs/abstracts, several unconfirmed)

- Linters separate shown-but-not-blocking tiers from blocking ones (Vale MinAlertLevel, ESLint warn/error; fetched).
- Prompt optimizers (OpenAI) have no "no changes" state at all; they always rewrite (fetched, vendor doc).
- Weak-supervision practice: fix a coverage gap with new rules, write them as patterns not copies of dev rows,
  keep one held-out set read once (Snorkel; not confirmed against the pages).
