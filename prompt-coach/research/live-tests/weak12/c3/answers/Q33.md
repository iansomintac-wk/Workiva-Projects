Draft Report Language

In FY2025, we engaged 120 key suppliers through our climate survey, receiving responses from 78 suppliers, representing 58% of our purchased goods spend. Of those responding, 31 have set emissions reduction targets, of which 12 have been validated by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi).
