Open Questions

Duplicate row for V-102 — Is this an intentional second entry or a data entry error? If a duplicate, totals will be overstated.
V-103 (EV with diesel usage) — Why does an EV have a diesel consumption figure? Is it a hybrid, or is the vehicle type mis-classified?
V-105 (ICE with electricity usage) — Why does an ICE vehicle show electricity consumption and no diesel? Is the vehicle type incorrect, or does this represent charging infrastructure usage?
Missing values — Several cells are blank (e.g., diesel for V-102, V-105; electricity for V-101, V-104). Are these true zeros or data not yet collected?
