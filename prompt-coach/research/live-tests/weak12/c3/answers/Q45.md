What May Have Driven the Increase
Air freight accounts for 61% of the total increase. The data attributes $45K of the $58K rise to a one-time expedited air event for a single customer recovery, which would suggest the underlying run-rate increase is only ~$13K.
Fuel surcharge reflects a discrete, carrier-imposed rate change (9% → 12% effective Aug 1), which is a structural cost increase unless renegotiated.
Ground freight rose moderately; no explanation is provided in the source data.
Warehousing was essentially flat.
