2. Approval Requirements

Table

Scenario	Required Approver

3. Due Diligence
Procurement must check every new vendor against the sanctions list prior to setup.
4. Vendor Master Maintenance
Master data is reviewed periodically (no specific frequency is defined — this could be a control gap worth noting in a SOX context).
5. Vendor Changes
Vendors can update address or contact details by emailing the AP inbox directly.
Potential Control Considerations
The undefined review frequency for vendor master data and the approval threshold overlap are two areas that may warrant attention if this policy is tied to any SOX controls or risk assessments in your GRC program.
