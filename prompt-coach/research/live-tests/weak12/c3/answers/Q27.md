Hypotheses (not confirmed by data)
Plant B Scope 1 surge (+1,260 tCO₂e, +203%): The noted refrigerant leak in July 2025 may account for a significant share of this increase, as refrigerant losses can carry high global-warming-potential multipliers. The leak may represent a one-time event rather than a structural change in operations.
Office C Scope 2 reduction (−315 tCO₂e, −77%): The switch to a renewable electricity tariff in March 2025 is consistent with the scale and timing of this reduction. The remaining ~95 tCO₂e may reflect consumption prior to the tariff switch or residual non-renewable supply.
Plant A modest reductions: The small decreases across both scopes could reflect efficiency improvements, lower activity levels, or mild weather variation — the data does not distinguish between these.
