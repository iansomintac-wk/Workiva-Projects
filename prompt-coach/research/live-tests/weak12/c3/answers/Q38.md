Supplier Engagement

Engaging our supply chain is a critical component of our approach to scope 3 emissions management. In FY2025, we expanded our supplier outreach by distributing our climate survey to 120 key suppliers, representing a significant portion of our purchased goods spend.

We received responses from 78 suppliers, achieving a response rate of 70%. Responding suppliers collectively account for 58% of our purchased goods spend, providing meaningful coverage of our supply chain emissions footprint.

Of the suppliers that responded, 31 have set emissions reduction targets, demonstrating growing climate ambition across our supplier base. Encouragingly, 12 of these suppliers have had their targets validated by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi), reflecting a commitment to science-aligned decarbonisation.

We continue to work collaboratively with our suppliers to support target-setting and emissions transparency, with the goal of increasing both engagement and the proportion of suppliers with validated science-based targets in future reporting periods.
