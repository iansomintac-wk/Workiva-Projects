Supplier Engagement

Engaging our supply chain is a critical component of our approach to reducing Scope 3 emissions. In FY2025, we extended our climate survey to 120 key suppliers, achieving a response rate of 70% (78 suppliers).

The responding suppliers represent 58% of our purchased goods spend, providing a meaningful view of emissions activity across a significant portion of our supply chain.

Of those that responded:

31 suppliers (40%) have set an emissions reduction target
12 suppliers (15%) have targets validated by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)

While we are encouraged by this progress, we recognise that further work is needed to drive broader target adoption across our supplier base. We will continue to support suppliers in setting credible, science-aligned targets and will use the insights from this survey to prioritise engagement activity in FY2026.
