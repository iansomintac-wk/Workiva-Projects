Two Primary Drivers

Air Freight (+$58K | ~61% of the variance) The largest single driver. $45K of the $58K increase is explicitly flagged as a one-time charge for expedited air shipping tied to a single customer recovery event. This should not recur in Q4.

Fuel Surcharge (+$14K | ~15% of the variance) The carrier surcharge rate was raised from 9% to 12% on August 1, a ~33% rate increase. Unlike the air freight spike, this is a structural/recurring change that will persist into Q4 unless renegotiated.

Key Takeaway

Roughly $45K of the $95K increase is one-time (expedited air). The remaining ~$50K reflects ongoing cost pressure, particularly from the fuel surcharge rate change. Worth flagging the fuel surcharge as a watch item for Q4 forecasting.
