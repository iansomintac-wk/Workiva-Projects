Q3 FY26 Freight Cost Increase — Key Drivers

Total freight costs rose $95K (+15%) from Q2 to Q3 FY26. Two items account for the vast majority of that increase:

Table

Cost Line	Q2 FY26	Q3 FY26	Change	Driver
Ground freight	$410K	$432K	+$22K	General volume/rate increase
Air freight	$60K	$118K	+$58K	One-time expedited air; customer recovery
Fuel surcharge	$38K	$52K	+$14K	Carrier rate raised 9% → 12% (Aug 1)
Warehousing	$120K	$121K	+$1K	Essentially flat
Total	$628K	$723K	+$95K	
Two Primary Causes

Air freight spike (+$58K) — The single largest driver. $45K of the $58K increase is flagged as a one-time charge tied to an expedited air shipment for a specific customer recovery event. This should not recur in Q4.

Fuel surcharge increase (+$14K) — The carrier raised its surcharge rate from 9% to 12% effective August 1, which is a permanent rate change and will carry forward into future quarters unless renegotiated.

Bottom Line

~77% of the quarter-over-quarter jump ($72K of $95K) is explained by these two items. The air freight portion is largely non-recurring, but the higher fuel surcharge represents a baseline cost increase going forward.
