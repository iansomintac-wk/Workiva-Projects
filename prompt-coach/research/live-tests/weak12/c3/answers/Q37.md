Observations (as hypotheses)
Plant B – Scope 1 surge (+1,260 tCO2e, +203%): The note references a refrigerant leak in July 2025. This may account for the majority of the increase, as refrigerants can carry very high global warming potentials; however, whether the full increase is attributable solely to that event has not been confirmed.
Office C – Scope 2 reduction (−315 tCO2e, −77%): The switch to a renewable electricity tariff in March 2025 likely explains most of this reduction. Because the switch occurred mid-year, a full-year renewable tariff could reduce this figure further in FY2026.
Plant A modest reductions (−90 and −60 tCO2e): These appear operationally stable; the small decreases may reflect efficiency improvements or lower activity, but no specific driver is noted in the data.
Plant B – Scope 2 slight reduction (−40 tCO2e): Consistent with general marginal improvement; no specific driver noted.
