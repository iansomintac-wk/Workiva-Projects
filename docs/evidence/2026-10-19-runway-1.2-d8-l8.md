## Step 4: decision

D8: outcome C (leave). L8: outcome C (leave).

Why not A: A needs 1.1 to have changed the behaviour on purpose. It did not: 1.0 and 1.1 fail identically and the code is byte-identical (H2). Rewriting the step to accept a still-listed confirmed card would change what the sim measures (the sim asks "does actioning a risk clear it from the open list?"), which is the question waiting on Ian's ruling.

Why not B: the page keeps confirmed cases on purpose (the confirmed card is the workspace for next steps; the toast says "next steps and the summary are open"; the spine counts it decided; the filter hides only dismissed). Calling that a bug and removing confirmed cases from the list is a product decision about where a confirmed risk's work lives, and it is Ian's open ruling, not a defect a test can prove.

So both stay as reported pre-existing findings: C1 1.1 FAIL [D8] and C2 1.1 FAIL [L8] are expected in the lead's full verify, and the same two fail on 1.0. L8's first half (product signal 0 -> 1 after the note) passes; only the "gone from the list" half fails, the same single condition as D8.

What would settle it: Ian's ruling on EXTENSION_PROGRESS item 3. "By design" -> outcome A for both steps (judge "decided" by the spine state or the `confirmed` class instead of absence). "Defect" -> outcome B (move confirmed cases out of the open list in the 1.1 html, with a red-first test on `renderRisk`).
