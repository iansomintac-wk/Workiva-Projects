**Hypothesis (assumed from reading code, not yet measured).** `sizeTrack` sets the track height to `sum(costOf of all cards but the last) + overflowOf(last) + stageForNatural(last) + 16`. `offsetForIndex(last)` (where a rail click scrolls to, and where `paint` makes the last card the front) is `sum(costOf of all cards but the last)`. The last card is reachable by scrolling only if `overflowOf(last) + stageForNatural(last) + 16 >= innerHeight` (only the page footer below the track can make up the rest). A SHORT last card (Fernhollow: little overflow, small stage) misses by about `innerHeight - stageForNatural(last) - 16`, so the page runs out of scroll (15684 in the repro) with Tidewater #28 still in front and Fernhollow `is-behind` (opacity ~0.84, one step behind: `1 - 0.26 * 0.62`).

Hypothesis says `needed > max`. If `needed <= max` it is DISPROVED: then call `scrollTo(needed)` directly, and compare each card's cost at bind time with its cost at click time (cards that grew after `sizeTrack` ran, e.g. a confirmed card opening its work area, are the next suspect).
