## The rule

A new Today source, `gone_quiet`, severity 4, added to `TODAY_SOURCE_DEFS` (1.1:11706) and emitted from `todayAccountCandidates` (1.1:11862). An account fires when it is not paused (`isPaused`, 1.1:6122) and one of these holds:

1. **Hand-off.** `implementation.wentLiveOn` is set (Go-Live, 1.1:6241), today is at least `GONE_QUIET_DAYS` (7) days after it, and no activity is dated after that day. The hand-off call itself, logged on the Go-Live day, does not stop it. No end date: it stops at the next activity.
2. **Pause return.** The account has an entry in `DATA.pauseReturns` (written by `clearExpiredPauses` when a pause lapses, 1.1:6155; pruned after 14 days, 6160), and no activity is dated on or after that entry's date. It fires from the entry's date with no wait, and stops at the next activity or when the tracker prunes the entry.

Activity means any logged activity in `DATA.activities[id]`, any type. The tracker has no direction field, and the probe measured identical results under an "only the customer's side" reading on all 81 measurements (measured).

`GONE_QUIET_DAYS = 7` is a constant beside the others, with no setting. The row's headline and reason name the trigger and its date, for example "No contact since hand-off on 5 Oct" or "Back from pause on 1 Dec, no contact yet". The exact words are a look call for Ian, made from the end-of-run captures.
