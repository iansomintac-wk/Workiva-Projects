# Pitch: "gone quiet" reaches Today (tracker 1.1)

Status: pitch, awaiting Ian's yes. Not a spec. The talk that follows turns it into one.
Source: day and lifecycle sim findings L7 and C3 (`docs/evidence/2026-10-16-osm-day-lifecycle-sim.md`), Ian's rulings 2026-10-19 (ledger `.ledger/2026-10-16-runway-day-sim/progress.md`).

## Problem

An account that goes silent never reaches the 15 rows the user actually sees on Today.

- C3, a new customer: hand-off call 2026-10-05, then 21 days of silence. It showed on day 1 only as a Gainsight gate card, which every onboarding customer gets. In the day's ranked list it sat 30th, past the 15-row cap, on 21 of 21 days (measured, both versions).
- L7, a customer back from a pause: on 12-01 the silence reasons came back (stalled, coverage neglected, session due), but it ranked 28th and was hidden (measured, both versions).

Why (read in 1.1 code, with two figures spot-checked against it): Today sorts by severity, 1 to 4 (`TODAY_SOURCE_DEFS` at 11707, `SIGNAL_DEFS` at 8100+, sort at 12034). Silence only rates 2 or 3, and its reasons fire late: stalled once days reach `riskAlertDays`, coverage neglected at 21 days (6913), session due at `defaultPaceDays` (8610). A hand-off or the end of a pause starts no reason of its own. So in a new customer's first week nothing says "quiet", and every severity-4 row and most severity-3 rows sort above it. The cap is `DAY_BEAT_MAX = 15` (12108). Rows past it go to Below the day, which shows 7 (`TODAY_VISIBLE_ROWS`, 11801) and puts the rest behind "show all".

Ian's ruling: a silent new account should reach the day's list within a week (C3, reading 2), and so should a silence that returns after a pause (L7).

## Appetite

Small batch, one run (Ian, 2026-10-19). If the fix cannot be kept that small, stop and re-shape. Do not dig deeper.

## Solution (approach A, Ian's pick)

Add one Today source, "gone quiet". It fires when a customer has had no activity for 7 days after its hand-off call, or 7 days after a pause ends, at severity 4 (the same as flagged). It stops firing at the customer's next activity. Everything else on Today stays as it is.

Questions for the talk to settle with a read-only probe on the sim book (34 accounts plus Fernhollow) before any number goes into the spec:
1. Which stored field is the hand-off date (implementationDate, or a logged Call)? Which marks a pause's end?
2. What counts as activity: any logged activity, or only one from the customer?
3. Flood check: on how many sim days, and for how many accounts at once, would "gone quiet" fire? If it is more than a few rows a day, it pushes other important rows out of the 15, and the pitch is re-shaped.

## No-gos

- No edits to 1.0. Tracker 1.1 only.
- No change to the 15 cap, the sort order, or any existing severity.
- No change to the Gainsight gate cards.
- No new settings screen. The 7 days is a constant beside the others.
- No test or synthetic data in a real book. Proof runs in the sim's own folders.

## Rabbit holes

- A flood of "gone quiet" rows on a busy book (probe question 3).
- Outbound-only contact (emails sent, no reply). Treat it as activity unless the probe shows that breaks C3.
- One account with both "gone quiet" and a later "stalled": the account's top candidate already takes the higher severity, so expect no double row (assumed; the talk checks it).
